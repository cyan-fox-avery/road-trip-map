Avery, Harley & Sarah's road trip

Not on the map

Every pin on Worth the Detour is a place we'd actually send you to. These are the ones we looked at and left off on purpose — and why. If a place isn't listed here, we probably just haven't looked at it yet.

Animal welfare comes first

We don't put a place on the map if it fails on animal welfare, no matter how famous it is. Tripadvisor flags these three as not meeting its Animal Welfare Policy, and we agree with the call.

  • Gatorland Orlando, Florida

    Flagged by Tripadvisor as not meeting its Animal Welfare Policy.

  • Georgia Aquarium Atlanta, Georgia

    The same flag — whale sharks, belugas and dolphins kept in captivity.

  • Shedd Aquarium Chicago, Illinois

    The same flag — beluga whales and dolphins kept in captivity.

Closed for good

No point sending you somewhere that isn't there any more.

  • Beyond the Lens! Family Fun Pigeon Forge, Tennessee

    Permanently closed in July 2026.

  • Old Apple Barn High Rolls, New Mexico

    Permanently closed and up for sale.

  • UFO Welcome Center Bowman, South Carolina

    Burned to the ground on May 9, 2024 — Jody Pendarvis's homemade flying-saucer landing pad is gone, and nothing remains to visit.

Didn't make the cut

We want lots of pins, but they have to be good ones — up against Dollywood, crazy restaurants, Meow Wolf and huge aquariums. These didn't hold up.

  • Kentucky Science Center Louisville, Kentucky

    Tripadvisor 3.8, with reviewers calling the exhibits tired and worn.

  • Museum of Ice Cream Chicago Chicago, Illinois

    Tripadvisor 3.1, reviewers call it overpriced and "not even a museum."

  • International Banana Museum Mecca, California

    Permanently closed — shut during COVID and never reopened, no plans to reopen ever.

  • South of the Border Hamer, South Carolina

    Tripadvisor 3.1 — the iconic I-95 roadside stop has faded: much of it closed, the Sombrero Tower not working, and mixed word on the Reptile Lagoon.

  • Museum of the Unknown St. Pete Beach, Florida

    $39–55 a person for a one-hour tour, with no Tripadvisor reviews at all.

  • St. Louis Union Station St. Louis, Missouri

    Tripadvisor 3.2 — reviewers call it an abandoned-mall feel. (The aquarium inside it is a different story.)

  • Branson Scenic Railway Branson, Missouri

    Tripadvisor 3.1 — "nothing scenic, just trees and rock walls."

  • Mississippi Aquarium Gulfport, Mississippi

    Tripadvisor 3.4 — small, and $35 a person at the gate.

  • Alien Research Center Hiko, Nevada

    Just a gift shop in a metal hut, not a research center.

  • Bison Paddock San Francisco, California

    Just a small herd in an 11-acre meadow.

  • Equine Affaire West Springfield, Massachusetts

    Only open four days a year.
